Upcoming events worth attending — DOD's own (announced via the [blog](/blog/)) plus organisations we track that have a public calendar feed (`ics_feed:`) or a manually confirmed upcoming event (`events:`). Subscribe to get new events automatically as they're added.

Election dates are here too — federal, state and local polling days across Australia, plus a selection of major elections elsewhere. They're the one kind of entry with no organisation behind it, so they get a violet card and a 🗳️ badge, and there's an [elections-only feed](/calendar-elections.ics) if that's all you want in your calendar. Where a date isn't settled yet the card says so: **expected date** (set by a fixed-term rule but not formally called), **due by this date** (the last day it can lawfully be held), or **day not yet set** (only the month is published). Listing an election is a statement about the date, not about the election.

This is a future-facing calendar of things to attend, not a historical record — see an organisation's own page for its history.
