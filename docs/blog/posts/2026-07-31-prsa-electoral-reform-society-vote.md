The [Proportional Representation Society of Australia (PRSA)](../../organisations/prsa.md) — DOD's Democracy Landscape entry describes it as "Australia's oldest electoral reform organisation, advocating for proportional representation via the Single Transferable Vote" — has circulated a formal notice to members ahead of its upcoming AGM proposing to rebrand as the **Australian Electoral Reform Society** and broaden its remit beyond STV/proportional representation specifically to cover a wider range of electoral-reform issues.

<!-- more -->

## What's being proposed

According to the notice circulated to members ([PDF](https://www.prsa.org.au/p3prsavt.pdf), [DOCX](https://www.prsa.org.au/p3prsavt.docx)) and a companion proposal document, members will be asked to vote on:

- Renaming the organisation to the Australian Electoral Reform Society
- Broadening its constitutional remit beyond proportional representation to cover other electoral-reform issues

*(This summary is drawn from a relayed community message describing the notice's contents, not a direct reading of the PDF — see the draft-review note above. Confirm the specifics against the primary document before publishing.)*

## How the vote works

PRSA's constitution reportedly requires a **75% majority** to adopt constitutional changes like this, and the society **does not allow proxy votes** — members need to attend (or otherwise formally participate in) the AGM directly for their vote to count. A member survey gauging support for the proposals is expected to go out in the weeks before the AGM.

**AGM date:** 12 November [year to be confirmed against the primary notice]

## Why it matters for DOD's Democracy Landscape

PRSA is already in DOD's Democracy Landscape as an electoral-reform advocacy organisation. A rebrand and remit expansion would be a significant structural change to an org DOD already tracks — worth updating the [PRSA entry](../../organisations/prsa.md) for once the outcome is known, regardless of which way the vote goes.

## Getting involved

Membership is reportedly $18/year, and joining before the AGM is what would let a new member's vote count toward the 75% threshold. Anyone interested should go directly to [PRSA's own website](https://www.prsa.org.au) to confirm current membership details and the AGM date rather than relying on this post.
