Folio Collective (trading as "Folio") is a Melbourne-based, member-supported forum that runs a recurring program of small-group public conversations — "Folio Conversations" — pairing diverse citizens with invited expert "Conversation Leaders" to discuss civic and democratic issues.[^home] Folio describes its purpose plainly: "Democracy evolves one conversation at a time. Let's have better conversations, more often."[^home] It positions itself as an independent, non-partisan, not-for-profit social enterprise that exists "to strengthen democracy through connected, citizen-led dialogue."[^event-state-of-nature]

## Format

Folio Conversations are deliberately small and capped: seats are "strictly limited to ensure that everyone has a meaningful opportunity to participate," with attendees encouraged to "bring their curiosity, actively listen, and share their perspectives and experiences."[^events] Each session pairs a themed civic or social question with one or more Conversation Leaders — subject-matter experts, practitioners, or advocates — for structured, small-group dialogue rather than a lecture-and-Q&A format.

Recent programming has run in themed clusters, including *Energy Shift* (the renewable energy transition's impact on regional communities), *State of Nature* (biodiversity and conservation), *Democratic Crossroads* (the 2023 Voice referendum and civic participation), *Election 2025* (nonpartisan analysis of the federal election), and *AI Futures* (AI governance).[^events]

## Membership and governance

Folio operates on a two-tier paid membership (Level 1 and Level 2, priced by expected in-person attendance frequency), plus casual/guest access to individual events.[^join-us] Members get first access and discounted tickets, an online member portal, and voting rights over "the future direction of the Folio Community."[^join-us] The organisation is run by a small operating team (Conversation Program Manager, Community Engagement Manager, Finance Manager, Marketing and Communications Manager) reporting to a volunteer Board, which Folio states should reflect "the diversity we want to see participating in our events" — explicitly welcoming Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people and people of every ethnicity, faith, ability, culture, language, age, gender identity, and sexual orientation as board candidates.[^team]

## Why it's on our radar

Folio sits in similar territory to DOD's own mission — nonpartisan, citizen-facing public dialogue on how society and government should work — but pursues it through a recurring in-person conversation series rather than a governance-design or advocacy focus. Past programming has covered accountability (Robodebt), electoral representation, referendum literacy (the Voice), and government AI policy, all squarely inside the Democracy Landscape's remit.[^events]
