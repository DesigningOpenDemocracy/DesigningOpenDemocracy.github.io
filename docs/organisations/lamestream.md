Lamestream is an independent Australian media outlet — podcast, YouTube show, and newsletter — [launched in April 2025](https://podnews.net/press-release/lamestream-podcast) by journalists Osman Faruqi and Scott Mitchell. It positions itself against the outlets it takes its name from ("lamestream" as a pejorative for legacy media), reporting on the state of Australian journalism itself rather than on politics directly.

Like [Constitutional Clarion](constitutional-clarion.md), it is a small, personality-led operation rather than a formal institution — included here on the same basis: DOD's interest is in the accountability work being done, not the size of the entity doing it. A functioning Fourth Estate is itself a democratic accountability mechanism, and outlets that report on whether that mechanism is working belong in the same landscape as outlets that report on constitutional or electoral mechanisms.

## What it covers

Per its own [launch press release](https://podnews.net/press-release/lamestream-podcast), the show was pitched around questions like "What does collapsing trust in legacy media mean for Australian society? Why has the ABC abandoned everyone under 60?" and the global rise of Trumpism and the far-right as it plays out in Australian media coverage.

- Ownership consolidation and political interference in Australian newsrooms
- Whether the ABC and commercial media are serving younger audiences
- Whether independent journalists and influencers can substitute for declining legacy journalism capacity
- Investigative reporting on Australian public figures and institutions — e.g. its [Epstein Files exclusive](https://www.lamestream.com.au/exclusive-how-israel-weapons-and-greed-drove-jeffrey-epsteins-mongolia-obsession/) on Jeffrey Epstein's advisory role to the Mongolian president, which also named former Australian PM Kevin Rudd as a fellow board member (Rudd denies meeting Epstein)
