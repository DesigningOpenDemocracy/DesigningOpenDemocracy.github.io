The Informal Urbanism Research Hub (InfUr-) is a group of researchers at the University of Melbourne's Melbourne School of Design. It describes itself as "an assemblage of researchers and projects focused on the role of urban informality in the creative production of cities of both the Global South and North."[^about]

Its governance angle is self-organisation. The hub says it "embodies a shared interest in how power is practised as a form of self-organisation, and in the struggles of marginalised populations to assert their right to the city."[^power] It treats informal urbanism as neither illegal nor separate from the state: it "intersects with the formal structures of state regulation and control."[^formal] That makes it a research counterpart to the Landscape's practitioners of participatory and co-operative governance. It studies how communities govern space when formal systems leave them out, and how co-design can share authority with them.

The hub runs a regular seminar series, and its co-directors are Kim Dovey, Crystal Legacy, Patrick Cobbinah and Ashraful Alam.[^codirectors]
