RadicalxChange Foundation is an international nonprofit and movement, founded by economist [E. Glen Weyl](https://glenweyl.com/) in 2018 following public discussion of his book *Radical Markets: Uprooting Capitalism and Democracy for a Just Society* (with Eric Posner). It grew from roughly fifty volunteers into a foundation that convened its first major conference in Detroit in March 2019.[^glenweyl][^propublica]

The foundation champions **Plurality**: a philosophy, and a book by Weyl and [Audrey Tang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audrey_Tang) — Taiwan's former Digital Minister — that treats technology as a means of cooperating across difference, rather than a force flattening human agency out of decision-making.[^rxc-about]

## Mechanisms

RadicalxChange's best-known contributions are two voting/funding mechanisms devised by Weyl:

- **Quadratic voting** — lets participants express the *intensity* of a preference, not just its direction, by spending a budget of "voice credits" whose cost per vote rises quadratically.
- **Quadratic funding** — a matching-funds formula for allocating public or philanthropic money toward public goods, weighting the number of contributors over the size of any single contribution.

Together with **Plural Ownership** and **Plural Money**, the foundation packages these as "the Plural Stack." Prototypes have been deployed in Colorado, Nashville, New York, the UK, Brazil, and Sydney.[^rxc-about]

## National-scale adoption

Two examples are cited as evidence the ideas move beyond pilots:

- **Taiwan** — quadratic-style civic tech and Pol.is-based deliberation (see [vTaiwan](vtaiwan.md)) informed by Audrey Tang, co-author of *Plurality*.
- **Japan** — Team Mirai, a party citing the Plurality framework, won parliamentary seats in the 2026 election.[^rxc-about]

## Key people

- **[E. Glen Weyl](https://glenweyl.com/)** — economist at Microsoft Research; founder, board member, and co-author of *Radical Markets* and *Plurality*.[^wiki-weyl]
- **Matt Prewitt** — Foundation President.[^rxc-about]
- **[Jess Scully](https://www.jessscully.com/about)** — Executive Director; previously an elected Councillor and Deputy Lord Mayor of Sydney, where she ran a citizens' jury on the city's 2030–2050 strategic plan; author of *Glimpses of Utopia: Real Ideas for a Fairer World*.[^rxc-jess]

## In Australia

RadicalxChange's Melbourne chapter is newly forming. A public launch event — "An invitation to meet RadicalxChange Foundation in Melbourne," hosted with Small Giants Academy and Today — is scheduled for 27 August 2026.[^humanitix] Jess Scully's connection to Melbourne runs through Small Giants Academy's [Wisdom & Action Forum](https://www.wisdomandaction.com.au/) (May 2026), where Audrey Tang also spoke.[^wisdom-action]
