The Better Politics Foundation (formerly Apolitical Foundation) is a Berlin-based, non-partisan global foundation co-founded by Lisa Witter — who also co-founded [Apolitical](apolitical.md) — to support political leaders directly, rather than the public servants Apolitical focuses on. Its own site describes it as the world's leading convener of political leadership entrepreneurs and innovators, serving as a convening space, a launchpad for new approaches and leaders, an amplifier giving a field visibility, and a connector linking people, resources, and innovation.

## What it does

"We focus on equipping courageous leaders with new tools and ideas to reimagine political systems."[^home] Its work includes the Better Politics Index and the Better Politics Fund (both listed in the org's own site navigation, though neither yet has a standalone page this session could verify content from). Current projects include a study of the mental health and wellbeing of politicians in Australia, a report on supporting newly elected Members of the German Bundestag through their transition into parliamentary work — "This report examines how newly elected Members of the German Bundestag can be better supported as they transition into parliamentary work"[^report] — and the annual Better Politics Week, held in Brussels in November 2026 after debuting in Berlin the prior year.

## Why it's on our radar

Lisa Witter appeared as a Conversation Leader at [Folio Collective](folio-collective.md)'s September 2026 event on democracy's present and future, introduced as co-founder and Executive Chair of Better Politics Foundation, "a global, non-partisan initiative dedicated to renewing leadership, reimagining politics, and rebuilding trust, which has helped spark 178 Political Leadership Incubators across 60 countries."[^folio-event] Where most of the Democracy Landscape focuses on citizens' participation mechanisms, the Better Politics Foundation works the other side of the same relationship — the quality, safety, and capability of the elected leaders citizens participate alongside.
