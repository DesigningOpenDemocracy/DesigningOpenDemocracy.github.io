Bank Australia is a customer-owned bank based in Collingwood, Victoria, tracing its origins to a 1957 CSIRO staff credit union. Over nearly seven decades — and roughly 72 mergers with other credit unions and co-operative banks — it grew into Bankmecu, Australia's first customer-owned bank, before adopting its current name in 2015.[^history]

## Governance

As a customer-owned (mutual) bank rather than a shareholder-owned one, Bank Australia's stated governance model gives every customer an equal ownership stake and vote:

> "Being customer owned means our customers have a voice and a vote on how the bank is run... Every customer has 1 equal share in Bank Australia. In other words, when you're a customer, you're also a shareholder... You get voting rights at our Annual General Meetings... As we're not listed on the stock exchange, we only answer to you."[^customer-owned]

Profits are returned to customers as "competitive rates, fair fees and quality products and services" rather than paid out as shareholder dividends or executive bonuses.[^customer-owned]

Bank Australia is a member of the Global Alliance for Banking on Values and a certified B Corporation.[^history] Antony McMullen — who has spoken at [DOD events on economic democracy and cooperatives](../blog/posts/2020-06-20-podcast.md) and co-founded [888 Co-operative Causeway](888-cooperative-causeway.md) and [Co-operative Bonds](cooperative-bonds.md) — has described Bank Australia as an example of the cooperative sector using its seat at the table to influence the wider banking industry, drawing on a conversation with the bank's Rowan Dowland at [DOD's inaugural Basil's Table event](../blog/posts/2020-02-20-podcast.md) in 2020.

## Recent structural change

In mid-2025, Bank Australia completed a merger with Qudos Bank, forming what the two banks described as one of the largest customer-owned banks in the country, with around 300,000 customers.[^merger] Bank Australia also completed the acquisition of Australian Unity's banking business in November 2025, and in May 2026 announced it was exploring a further potential merger with P&N Bank.[^history] Consolidation among mutuals raises the same governance question DOD tracks for any growing member-owned institution: whether one-member-one-vote control and local accountability survive scale — a question this page will keep watching rather than assume settled by the customer-owned label alone.
