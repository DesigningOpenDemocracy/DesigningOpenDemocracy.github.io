# Landscape News

<!-- An explicit heading because the nav tab is just "News", and with no
     heading of its own Material prints the nav label as the page title. -->

What organisations across the Democracy Landscape have published, launched or announced over the past three months: new reports and handbooks, platform launches, leadership changes, rulings and results. Subscribe by RSS, or narrow it to a country or topic. Conferences and meetups are on the [calendar](calendar.md).

<summary>About this page — what counts as news, and where it comes from</summary>
<p>News here means something that happened: a publication, a launch, an appointment, a ruling, a result. Things people attend (conferences, forums, meetups, AGMs) are the <a href="/calendar/">calendar</a>'s, and stay off this page once they're over. The one exception is a heads-up: a <strong>major</strong> conference or summit appears under "Coming up" for the month before it starts, and leaves when it ends. Some items are both, like a report launched at a public webinar: those are on the calendar until they happen and here afterwards.</p>
<p>Every item is an entry on an organisation's own page in the <a href="/organisations/">Democracy Landscape</a> that an editor has flagged as <strong>major</strong> (★, rare and flagship-scale) or <strong>notable</strong> (worth calling out). It carries the same citation as the organisation's timeline, and the headline links straight to the source. Anything older than three months is in the <a href="/archive/">Landscape Archive</a> and on the organisation's own page. This is deliberately not a feed of everything every organisation publishes, so it's short on purpose.</p>
<p>There's an RSS feed for everything, one per country and one per topic (all listed under "All news feeds" below), and a <a href="/news.json">JSON Feed</a> for anything that reads structured data. Topics are the concepts each organisation works on, so a topic feed follows organisations in that field.</p>
